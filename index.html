BSc Thesis · Product Development · MF132X

E-ATX — Outdoor Strength Training Machine

Developed in collaboration with Gymkompaniet and its brand Recoil. Extensive pre-study across market, trends and users — leading to three concepts evaluated through structured selection. The final concept, E-ATX, is a modular half-rack with two integrated weight stacks, internally routed cables in adjustable arms, and a non-structural outer shell for weather protection. Designed to enable versatile progressive strength training outdoors, year-round in Nordic climates, without compromising durability or safety.

FEM / Simulation · SD2175

Room Acoustics of a Residential Apartment

2D COMSOL finite element model of a real apartment floor plan. Studies eigenfrequencies, door-state coupling, sub-millimetre gap leakage, road-noise excitation, and passive bass-trap treatment.

Additive Manufacturing · MG2044

Topology Optimization for Additive Manufacturing

Investigates the effect of AM constraints on topology optimization of a Ti-6Al-4V hanger bracket in ANSYS Workbench. Compares a weight-only optimized design against an LPBF-constrained design with 45° overhang and anisotropic elasticity — revealing trade-offs between mass reduction and physical printability.
